No hidden assumptions

Three methods, one clear formula

Fermentation recipes do not all use the same basis for a salt percentage. Pick the method stated by your source—these methods are not interchangeable.

01

Dry-salted

Often used for shredded vegetables that release their own liquid. The percentage is applied to the weight of the vegetables or other ingredients specified by the recipe.

salt weight = ingredient weight × percentage ÷ 100
02

Water-only brine

Some recipes define their brine percentage using only the water. Enter the water weight—not jar capacity or an estimate of the finished ferment.

salt weight = water weight × percentage ÷ 100
03

Total ingredients

Some recipes base salt on the combined weight of the vegetables and water. We add those weights first, then apply the recipe’s percentage.

salt weight = (ingredients + water) × percentage ÷ 100
i

Why we don’t recommend a percentage: appropriate salt amounts depend on the specific food and tested recipe. The National Center for Home Food Preservation notes that salt is important to the safety and texture of fermented sauerkraut and brined pickles, and cautions against reducing it. This calculator only performs the arithmetic for a percentage you already have from a reliable recipe.

A better batch starts before the jar

Small details make the math useful.

A precise number only helps when the inputs and method match the recipe. Use these checks before you weigh.

1

Check the recipe’s basis

Confirm whether its percentage refers to vegetables, water, or the combined ingredients. If it is not clear, consult the recipe author or a trusted extension service rather than guessing.

2

Weigh in compatible units

Use a scale and enter ingredient and water weights separately. The calculator converts each to grams before applying the formula.

3

Use the salt called for

Use the salt type and amount specified by your tested recipe. Salt crystal sizes and spoon volumes differ, so weigh it when the recipe provides a weight.

4

Follow the whole process

Salt math does not replace clean handling, the right container, fermentation temperature, submersion, storage instructions, or any recipe-specific safety steps.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Which method should I choose?

Use the basis specified in your recipe. The calculator cannot determine the intended method from the food name alone, and switching methods changes the result.

Does this tell me whether a ferment is safe?

No. It calculates salt weight only. Food safety also depends on the recipe, ingredients, preparation, fermentation conditions, and storage. Use a current, tested recipe and follow all of its instructions.

Can I use a lower salt percentage?

Do not use this calculator to reduce salt in a fermented-food recipe. The National Center for Home Food Preservation warns that salt is important to the safety and texture of fermented sauerkraut and brined pickles.

Does the calculator save my recipe?

No. Calculations run in your browser. This page does not send entered weights to a server or save them for later.
