Privacy policy

What this site does—and does not do—with information about your visit.

Last updated: November 1, 2026.

Information you enter in the calculator

The fermentation calculator runs in your browser. In this version, the ingredient weights, water weights, and percentage you enter are used to calculate a result on your device; they are not sent to Ferment Math or stored by the site.

Information you send us

If you contact us by email, your email address and the contents of your message will be processed by your email provider and the site operator so we can respond. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information. We retain correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to handle your request or meet applicable legal obligations.

Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. Our hosting provider may process technical information, such as your IP address and request data, to deliver and secure the website.

If you see ads on this website, they may be provided by third-party vendors, including Google. These vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Google and its partners may use advertising cookies to personalize ads based on your visits to this website or other websites. Ad personalization depends on your settings, location, consent, and applicable law.

You can manage Google’s personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also visit aboutads.info choices to learn about opting out of personalized advertising from participating third-party vendors.

Consent and your choices

Where consent is required by law or by an advertising platform’s rules, the site will obtain the required consent before using relevant non-essential cookies or serving personalized advertising. Google requires publishers serving personalized ads to users in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland to use a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF). See Google’s consent-management requirements.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect features offered by third-party services.

Third-party websites

This site links to external resources, including food-preservation references and Google settings. Those sites have their own privacy policies and practices. We are not responsible for their content or handling of information.

Children’s privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address the request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when site features, advertising providers, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates when this page was most recently revised. Please review it periodically.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our Contact page.
